My Hobbies

My hobbies cover sports, reading, music, animation and history. They balance my heavy engineering study and help me keep a rich inner world.

02

Football

Football is my favorite sport. I play two positions in the university football team: full-back and centre forward. As a full-back, I focus on defense and wing assist; as a centre forward, I try to seize scoring chances.

My favorite football player is Dani Alves. I admire his excellent athletic ability, positive attitude and outstanding awareness on both defense and attack.

03

Reading & History

I love reading history and military books. My favorite book is The Global Histories. I often watch history documentaries and classic historical materials on Bilibili and Douyin, especially the history of World War II. These contents help me understand the evolution of human society and the logic behind wars and development.

I also keep the habit of writing. Writing allows me to sort out my thoughts and record my opinions and feelings.
