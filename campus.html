About University of Emergency Management

University of Emergency Management is China’s first university focusing on emergency management, directly under the Ministry of Emergency Management. It is located in Sanhe, Langfang, Hebei Province. The school takes disaster prevention, risk reduction and emergency rescue as its core running direction, covering engineering, safety, emergency management and many other disciplines. It focuses on cultivating practical, innovative talents who can face all kinds of safety risks and disasters.
